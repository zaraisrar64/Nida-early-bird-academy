Welcome, families

About Us

Early Bird Academy is a fictional early learning community created to help children feel safe, valued, and excited to learn. Our welcoming classrooms are designed for discovery, friendship, creativity, and growing independence. We partner with families because children thrive when the important adults in their lives work together.

Program Director: Nida Zohra

Our Mission

At Early Bird Academy, our mission is to provide high-quality early childhood education in a nurturing environment where children can learn, explore, and thrive. We believe in fostering curiosity, independence, creativity, and respect while building strong relationships with families and the community.

Our Vision

Every child is known, respected, and inspired to reach their potential in a supportive learning community.

Our Approach

We balance child-led exploration with intentional teaching, predictable routines, and strong family partnerships.

Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are your hours?

We are open Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. The center is closed on selected holidays, which are shared with families in advance.

2. How do I arrange a tour or enroll my child?

Contact the director by phone or email to schedule a tour. After the visit, interested families receive an enrollment packet, health and emergency forms, and the family handbook.

3. Are meals and snacks provided?

Yes. The program provides a morning snack, lunch, and an afternoon snack. Families should share allergies, dietary restrictions, and cultural food practices before the child's start date.

4. How do teachers communicate with families?

Teachers share daily updates at pickup and through a secure family communication platform. A weekly email includes reminders, classroom learning, and upcoming events.

5. What happens if my child is sick?

Please keep your child home when they have a fever, vomiting, diarrhea, or symptoms that prevent comfortable participation. Contact the director for the illness and return-to-care guidelines in the family handbook.

6. Do children go outdoors every day?

Outdoor play is part of our daily plan when weather and safety conditions allow. Please send clothing suitable for the season.

7. How do you support children with different needs?

We work with families to understand each child's strengths, routines, communication, and support needs. When appropriate, we collaborate with families and relevant specialists to plan inclusive participation.
